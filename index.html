JavaScript Practice Assignment

Question 1: First and Last Name

Create two variables, firstName and lastName, and print your full name.

Question 2: Arithmetic Operations

Create three variables, num1, num2, and num3. Perform addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

Question 3: Temperature Checker

Take temperature from the user. If it is 0°C or below, print "Freezing". Otherwise, print "Not freezing".

Question 4: Age Category

Take the user's age and display Child, Teenager, Adult, or Senior Citizen.

Question 5: Electricity Bill Calculator

Calculate the electricity bill using cumulative rates: first 100 units Rs. 10 per unit, next 100 units Rs. 15 per unit, and above 200 units Rs. 20 per unit.

Question 6: Traffic Light

Take a traffic light color and print Stop, Ready, Go, or Invalid Color.

Question 7: Pocket Money Checker

If pocket money is greater than Rs. 500, print "You can buy a gift". Otherwise, print "Save your money".

Question 8: Free Delivery Checker

If shopping amount is Rs. 2000 or more, print "Free Delivery". Otherwise, print "Delivery Charges Apply".
